Brownies!!

Ingredients:

  • baking spray
  • 2 cups white sugar
  • 1 and 1/4 cups all purpose flour
  • 1 cup unsalted butter, unmelted
  • 4 large eggs
  • 1/2 cup cocoa powder
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 cup walnut halves

Steps:

  1. Preheat the oven to 350 degrees F (175 degrees C). Grease a 9x13-inch pan with baking spray.
  2. Whisk sugar, flour, melted butter, eggs, cocoa powder, vanilla, baking powder, and salt in a large bowl until combined.
  3. Spread the batter into the prepared pan.
  4. Decorate with walnut halves.
  5. Bake in the preheated oven until top is crinkled and a toothpick comes out with a few moist crumbs, about 20 to 30 minutes.
  6. let cool completely in the pan on a wire rack before slicing into squares. Enjoy!
